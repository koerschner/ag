## Rubric (grade every item pass/fail, with times)

**Truth**
1. Every claim in the narration matches the product's real rules and names (checked in code or docs, not guessed).
2. Numbers on screen agree with the narration and with each other (no real screenshot contradicting a drawn meter).
3. No test data, dev badges, placeholder art, broken images, or other people's real names/faces that shouldn't be there.

**Sync**
4. Each line's picture shows what the voice is saying at that moment (check `cues.jpg`); key reveals land on their word, within ~0.3 s.
5. No scene sits empty or static waiting for its cue for more than ~0.8 s; holds keep a slow drift.

**Layout**
6. Nothing overlaps unintentionally: mascot, tags, titles, windows and captions each have their own space.
7. Content stays out of the caption zone (bottom ~130 px) and keeps >= 60 px from the frame edges; no awkward empty halves of the frame.
8. Every word on screen is readable at 1280 px wide: large type, real screens zoomed or cropped to the part that matters.

**Craft**
9. One idea per scene; the viewer always knows where to look (motion, ring, or zoom points at it).
10. Consistent design system: same fonts, colors, corner radii, tag and title styles throughout; brand matches the product.
11. Motion is smooth and purposeful (ease-out entrances, no jitter, no element popping in without a transition).
12. Captions are on one or two lines, in sync, and never cover something important.

**Sound**
13. Narration clear and natural (report shows a 1.00-ish match per line; listen to any flagged line).
14. Loudness about -16 LUFS, true peak below -1 dBFS; music audible but under the voice; SFX on on-screen moments, none harsh.

**Story**
15. A first-time viewer could explain the concept after one watch: who it's for, the 2-4 steps, and the one thing to do next.
