# Style: product walkthrough (master prompt)

For: teaching a new user how a product works, using its real screens.

- Look: the product's own dark/light theme, brand fonts and colors. Background a soft gradient
  with slow drifting texture (the product's own art if it has some). Real screens sit in rounded
  "windows" with a soft shadow and a thin brand-colored rim; components (a meter, a chat panel,
  a dialog) float as cards.
- Structure: hello (logo + mascot or presenter) -> "here's how it works" (the 2-4 steps as chips)
  -> one scene per step (concept drawn big, then the real screen proving it) -> edge case
  reassurance -> recap the steps as three big words -> logo + the one next action.
- Persistent HUD: small logo + step pills top-left, the current step lit.
- Concepts get a drawn, oversized version first (a meter filling, a counter ticking, tokens
  flying into it), then cut to the real UI so viewers recognise it in the app.
- Point at things: gold ring around the button being described, a tag naming each panel,
  push-in on the part of a screen that matters. Never show a whole dense screen at small size.
- Mascot appears at the start, can point at the step map, and celebrates at the end; not in
  every scene.
- Captions always on (karaoke, current word bright), bottom center.
- SFX: the product's own UI sounds on the matching moments (reward on XP, unlock on a goal,
  message sounds on chat, join sound on a call), soft whooshes on scene changes.
