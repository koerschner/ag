## Arcade Discord

Use Discord for messages to the user's team unless they say otherwise; a teammate's "brain channel" is their named channel there. **Never post in anyone's brain channel**; post in `#dev` or DM the person instead. Read and post with the **`ag discord`** CLI (the ag bot, on ag-engine; no computer use): load the **`arcade-discord`** skill first (threads, replies, attribution). Never route Discord through the client Mac; the only fallback is the Discord app on ag-mac. The server, team handles, and other specifics are in the user's private personal instructions (`~/AGENTS.md`).
