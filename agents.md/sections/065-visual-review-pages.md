## Visual review pages

Whenever work changes something people see (UI screens, dialogs, native alerts, emails, dashboards, copy), give the user a review page before asking them to approve it: load the **`visual-review-page`** skill and follow it. Screenshots in chat alone aren't enough.

For flows, record video too: `ag-record-flow` (headless Playwright) or `ag-screen-record start|stop` (ag-mac's desktop during a CUA run); see the skill.
