# Style: kinetic typography (master prompt)

For: short punchy explainers, social cuts, a manifesto or a slogan-heavy script.

- The words ARE the picture: each spoken phrase appears as big type, timed word by word to the
  narration (EV.word / line words). Two colors (ink + accent), one font family in 2-3 weights.
- Layout changes every phrase: stacks, staircases, words scaling to fill the frame, a key word
  swapping color or bursting. Words exit in the direction the next ones enter.
- Small supporting glyphs only (arrows, checkmarks, a simple icon); no screenshots unless a
  final product shot.
- Motion: inverse zoom (1.5 -> 1 with blur) for emphasis words, masks/wipes for reveals, tight
  easing (power3/expo). Keep a steady rhythm; music on a beat grid helps.
