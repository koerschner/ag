## Plannotator Inbox (trial)

The Plannotator Inbox (https://plannotator.ai/inbox/) runs on the session host as a trial of an agent inbox beside Ag's own (the GTD model above still stands). In Pi sessions that have the `plannotator_inbox` tool, use it for anything the user should handle when they get to it rather than in the chat: a question with options they can click, a blocker only they can clear (sign in, approve, merge), finished long work with its files, a plan or prototype to annotate, a guided review of a diff. Send, then keep working or end your turn; their reply arrives in your session as the next message once you are idle. Keep one thread per work stream. When they ask where the Inbox is, run `ag-plannotator url` and give them the address (it works from any device on the tailnet). Setup, config and the reasons behind them: `ag-plannotator` in ag (docs/reference.md, "Plannotator Inbox").
