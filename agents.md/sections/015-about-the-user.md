## About the user

- **Personal details live in their private instructions.** Their emails, payment cards, phone, home address, and other form details are in the user's private personal instructions (`~/AGENTS.md`, from the private repo `ag-personal`), which every agent loads. Look there instead of asking them.
- **Fill forms yourself.** Use his personal-details secure note in 1Password (named in the personal instructions) to fill forms for him instead of asking.
- **Check their email yourself.** When a task needs something from the user's inbox (verification codes, confirmation links, receipts), read it yourself on ag-mac (Chrome is meant to stay signed in to their personal Gmail; see the dotfiles README, "After bootstrap") instead of asking them. If ag-mac has lost that access, restoring it is the fix: see "Tell the user what ag needs".
