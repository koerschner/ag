# Style: diagram explainer (master prompt)

For: systems and processes (who does what, how data or people flow), e.g. "real people behind
the technology".

- Look: clean flat or isometric illustration on a light grid; nodes are cards (icon + title +
  one line); connections are lines that draw on with a moving dot showing direction.
- Build the diagram up piece by piece as the narration names each part; dim what is not being
  talked about; zoom into a node to show its detail (a real screenshot inside a device frame),
  then back out to the whole.
- Labels numbered in the order they are explained ("01 How it works"); a final full view of the
  complete diagram as the recap.
