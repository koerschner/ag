# Style: Vox-style explainer (master prompt)

For: explaining an idea or a model (why it works, how the pieces fit), not a UI tour.

- Look: textured paper/cream background (subtle noise + grain), black type, one accent color
  (orange or the brand's), occasional second accent. Cut-out photos and real images with a white
  sticker border and a slight drop shadow, as if pasted on the page. Hand-drawn style arrows,
  circles and underlines that draw themselves (SVG stroke-dashoffset tweens).
- Type: bold condensed headline font, all caps for key words; short labels; numbers huge.
- Motion: things slide on and settle with a little overshoot; the camera pushes and pans across
  a big "page" (one large div you translate/scale) rather than cutting; highlighter swipes behind
  key words; charts build bar by bar.
- Use external images (photos, screenshots, maps) generously; they carry the credibility.
- Every 3-5 seconds something new arrives; no long static holds.
